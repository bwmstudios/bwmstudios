FAQ

Questions Business Owners Always Ask Us

How long does it take to build my site?

Most sites are live within 7 days. For the Dominator package with all integrations, allow up to 10–12 days for the best result.

What do I need to provide?

Just your logo (if you have one), some photos of your work or space, your services and prices, and your contact details. We guide you through everything else.

Can I update the site myself after launch?

Yes — we can set up a simple content management system. Or message us any time and we'll make the update for you, usually within 24 hours.

Does the booking system charge commission?

No. We integrate a booking system that's either free or very low cost (under 50 AED/month) — all bookings go directly to you with zero commission.

What if I don't have professional photos?

No problem. Good phone photos work well. We can advise on how to take better shots yourself, or recommend a local photographer if you want pro results.

Will the website help me rank on Google?

The Growth and Dominator packages include SEO setup that significantly improves your Google ranking. The Google Business Profile setup alone can put you on the local map within weeks.

Can I start with Starter and upgrade later?

Absolutely. Many clients start with Starter to get online fast, then upgrade to Growth once they see the results. Upgrade pricing is fair — you only pay the difference.

Do you work with all types of local businesses?

Yes — salons, clinics, restaurants, gyms, law firms, cleaning services, mechanics, photographers, tutors, and more. If you serve customers locally in Dubai, we can build your website.
