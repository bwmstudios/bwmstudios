FAQ

Questions Salon Owners Always Ask Us

How long does it take to build my site?

Most salon and barbershop sites are live within 7 days. For the Full Booster package with all integrations, allow up to 10–12 days for the best result.

What do I need to provide?

Just your logo (if you have one), some photos of your shop and your work, your services and prices, and your contact details. We guide you through everything else.

Can I update the site myself after launch?

Yes — we can set up a simple content management system. Or you can message us any time and we'll make the update for you, usually within 24 hours.

Does the booking system charge commission?

No. We integrate a booking system that's either free or very low cost (under 50 AED/month) — all bookings go directly to you with zero commission taken.

What if I don't have professional photos?

No problem. Good phone photos work well. We can also advise you on how to take better shots yourself, or recommend a local photographer if you want professional results.

Will the website help me rank on Google?

The Growth and Full Booster packages include SEO setup that significantly improves your Google ranking. The Google Business Profile setup alone can put you on the map for local searches within weeks.

How much is the monthly maintenance?

The Monthly Retainer starts at 250 AED/month. This covers health checks, backups, 2 content updates and domain renewal management. Extra updates are 100–150 AED each.

Can I start with Starter and upgrade later?

Absolutely. Many clients start with Starter to get online fast, then upgrade to Growth once they see the results. Upgrade pricing is fair — you only pay the difference.
